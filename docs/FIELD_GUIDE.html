ZOOM 0

The north star — a quality signal that survives having no reference

PhD frame: "Perceptually Grounded Codecs and Reward Models for Generative Audio."

Every classical audio-quality metric (PESQ, ViSQOL) compares a degraded signal against its clean original. Generated audio has no original. There is nothing to diff against — which is why generative models can sound subtly wrong in ways no automated signal catches.

The bet: a differentiable, no-reference metric — the "wheel" — that reads quality from the audio itself. Differentiable matters because then a generator can be trained against it (a reward model). The wheel is first built and calibrated on known distortions (where we can verify it), then scaled toward generated audio (where nothing else works).

ZOOM 1

Pilot A's wager — the information may already be there, for free

Before training anything: check whether pretrained models already did the work.

Neural audio codecs (EnCodec, DAC, Mimi) and self-supervised speech models (WavLM) were trained on enormous audio corpora for other purposes — compression, representation learning. The wager: their internal representations (latents) already organize degradation information geometrically, so that tiny linear probes can read out what kind of damage and how much — with no reference signal, and without training the encoders at all (they stay frozen).

Five pre-registered research questions: RQ1 is type/severity linearly readable? RQ2 do combined distortions add up geometrically? RQ3 is severity monotone in latent space (more damage → further along a direction)? RQ4 can a small head on frozen latents predict perceptual quality scores? RQ5 does any of it interpolate to unseen severities?

Either answer is a result: yes → the wheel's embryo exists for cheap; no → codecs discard perceptual damage structure, which itself redirects the thesis.

ZOOM 2

The experiment — controlled damage, free labels

You can't learn to read damage unless you know exactly what the damage was.

Corpus

510 clean speech clips · 102 VCTK speakers × 5 clips · 4–10 s · loudness-normalized to −23 LUFS. Splits are speaker-disjoint: a speaker is entirely in train or entirely in test, never both — otherwise the probe could cheat by recognizing voices.

Known caveat: the selected clips concentrate in VCTK's shared passages, so train and test speakers read the same sentences. This makes the pilot content-controlled (good: content can't explain differences) but forbids any content-generalization claim. Logged in DECISIONS.md; embedded in the report itself.

The two guard baselines (what makes this science, not vibes)

  • logmel — a plain mel spectrogram. If it probes as well as the codecs, the codecs add nothing over classical DSP: the floor.
  • energy — 3-band loudness only. If severity is readable from this, "severity" was just volume: the control.

What each of tonight's ~338k cached files went through.

 │        one real-valued vector per time step — no phase, no complex numbers

 │        (frame-level kept only for dropout — a 20 ms glitch

So "the latent" at probe time is the pooled clip vector: 128–2048 numbers summarizing one clip under one condition. That vector is the point your shift-vector geometry (Δ = zdirty − zclean) lives on.

ZOOM 4

The gates — pre-registered, fail-closed, allergic to self-deception

Fixed before any real data existed. A FAIL is a finding, never a bug to tune away.

Gate 1 — "it's not just loudness"

For each of the 17 candidates: severity rank-correlation (Spearman, bootstrap CIs over speaker groups) must beat the energy control by a CI-separated margin, AND type macro-F1 must clear the logmel floor. Underpowered reports (too few test groups) refuse to render a verdict at all.

Gate 2 — "it tracks perception, not another metric's quirks"

A ridge head on frozen latents predicts ViSQOL (G2a), then must beat the no-reference incumbents (NISQA/DNSMOS/UTMOS) on human MOS held away from training (G2b). A rig-guard rejects a head that merely parrots ViSQOL's ranking. No MOS available → G2b says "not evaluable" — it never substitutes.

The honesty machinery

  • Provenance: every artifact stamps fake:true/false, derived from which encoders ran — a plumbing-check JSON cannot impersonate a result.
  • Embedded caveats: tonight's report carries its own warnings inside the file (winner unconfirmed on a held-out split; content-controlled corpus).
  • Frozen gates: thresholds, candidate list, and split logic predate data contact.
ZOOM 5

Tonight, tomorrow, and your experiment

Tonight: six worker processes (one per encoder) render + encode all 18,360 waveforms into the cache; then Gate 1 runs over all 17 candidates → reports/gate1_real.json: per-candidate PASS/fail, margins over floor and energy, test-group power, caveats, provenance fake:false — the repo's first real numbers.

Then — the shift-vector experiment (yours): for each clip, Δ = z(dirty) − z(clean). Ask: do all 510 Δ's for one distortion point the same way (mean pairwise cosine vs the 1/√D random-baseline)? Does ‖Δ‖ grow monotonically with severity? Do the 7 family directions stay distinguishable after PCA — measured in full dimension first, reduction only to test persistence? A consistent direction per family = a linear distortion axis = the wheel's first spoke: correction is projection-and-subtraction, and projections are differentiable.

Queued after: Gate 2 metric tables (ViSQOL/PESQ build, NR incumbents, eventually human MOS), the winner-confirmation split, a content-general corpus arm (VCTK utt ≥025), music/sound arms.
