First Light

The first real-corpus results: 338,130 latents, Gate 1's frozen verdict, and the answer to the shift-vector question. Every number below is from reports/gate1_real.json and reports/geometry_real.json (2026-08-22, RTX 5070, real backends only). Every figure is real data.

1 · What ran

VCTK 510 clips (102 speakers x 5)
   -> 7 damage families x 5 severities + clean  (36 conditions, rate-dependent)
   -> 6 encoders, 18 candidate representations  -> 338,130 cached latent cells
   -> Gate 1: linear probes, frozen thresholds, fail-closed   (55.7 min)
   -> RQ2 geometry: 18-candidate shift-vector sweep           (49.1 min)

Speaker-grouped splits (14,760 train / 3,600 test rows, 20 test speakers), corpus-global headroom, content-addressed resume-safe cache. Provenance embedded; zero fake backends.

20 test groups vs a minimum of 3 — nobody is "underpowered". This is a clean fail, which is exactly what a fail-closed gate is for: FAIL is a finding.

The decomposition is the story:

· WavLM's early layers read WHAT. l1/l6 are the only representations that beat spectral statistics at damage-type ID, CI-separated. And type-F1 decays monotonically with depth — l1 0.988 → l24 0.906, landing exactly on the mel floor — the pre-registered prediction that deeper SSL layers abstract toward phonetics and legally discard damage.

· EnCodec comes closest on HOW MUCH. 3/7 severity families at every depth — and its absolute dose-tracking is strong (noise .980, hum .977, hiss .952 SRCC). What's thin is the margin over the loudness-only control: much of "severity" is energy, and the gate demands the residual beyond it.

· Mimi's semantic path is near-blind (0.402 vs its own acoustic path's 0.862) — the pathway distilled to keep meaning and discard acoustics does exactly that.

· Nobody reads both. The SSL-vs-codec training-pressure split (predict context vs reconstruct waveform) shows up as a clean division of labor — itself thesis material.

3 · The shift-vector experiment: the axes are real

The original question: many clean clips + one identical distortion → per-clip shift vectors Δ = z(dirty) − z(clean) — do the directions agree, does length track dose, and does any of it survive dimension reduction? The honest yardstick: in dimension D, two random directions sit at cos ≈ 0 ± 1/√D — every bar below is measured against that null.

wavlm:l1 — per-family mean pairwise cos(Δ), null = 0.022 (D=2048). Median family 0.65 ≈ 29x the null.

mimi:semantic — same plot, same corpus: median 0.069, barely 2x its null. The damage-blind pathway has no damage geometry. A negative control that behaves is worth as much as a positive result.

wavlm:l1 condition centroids on PC1×PC2 — each point is one (family, severity) cell's centroid; color = family, size = severity. Families fan out along distinct directions with severity ordered along each ray: direction = diagnosis, length = dose, drawn by the data itself.

encodec24k:z — median ||Δ|| vs severity per family. Monotone in every family (SRCC .71–.98). This is the "severity wheel" reading, pre-probe: the raw geometry already knows the dose.

encodec24k:z — cosine matrix of family mean-directions. The additive-noise trio (noise/hiss/hum) clusters — physically similar damage, similar directions — while structured damage (clip, dropout, mp3, bandlimit) stays more separable. Off-diagonal structure is what Phase 7's additivity test now probes directly.

4 · Reconciling the two results

Gate 1 says FAIL; the geometry says the axes are vivid. Both are true, because they measure different things. The gate scores margins over strong baselines — and the baselines have geometry too: log-mel's own shift vectors concentrate at 11.7x its null. Damage directions existing in latent space was never the hard part; the gate asks whether frozen deep latents beat cheap spectral statistics at reading them, and (except WavLM l1/l6 on type) they don't — on this corpus.

One line to keep: the gate graded the race; the geometry photographed the track. The track is real — wavlm:l1 runs it best on every instrument at once (type 0.988, tightest directions, most consistent dose, best 2-D persistence).

5 · What bounds these claims

Caveats, embedded in both artifacts: (1) content-controlled corpus — train/test speakers read the same VCTK passages, which likely flatters the mel floor; the content-general arm (utt ≥ 025) runs before any public negative. (2) No winner-confirmation split yet — wavlm:l1's crown is unconfirmed on held-out data. (3) Geometry is descriptive and in-sample by design; cosines live in the standardized basis; the 1/√D null assumes isotropy; ||Δ|| is never comparable across candidates.

6 · Phase 7: two artifacts at once

The decomposition question: stack two damages on the same clip (order fixed A-then-B) and ask (a) does the combo shift vector superpose — Δ(a+b) ≈ Δa + Δb — and (b) does the type probe, trained only on single damages, read the combo zero-shot? Three pairs (noise+clip, hiss+bandlimit, hum+mp3) at severities 2–4; 141-minute run over all 18 candidates (reports/combos_real.json).

Independent artifacts superpose everywhere. noise+clip and hum+mp3 land at additivity cosine +0.98..+1.00 in every candidate at every severity — curved codec latents included. Log-mel's +0.989 mean is the linear ceiling, and everyone matches it on these pairs: superposition of non-interacting damages is a property of the signal, not a discriminator between representations. Good news for the wheel — independent axes decompose safely in any of these latents.

The interacting pair is where the story is. hiss then bandlimit is physically non-additive — the low-pass erases the hiss band. The latents expose this, graded by severity:

encodec z collapses 0.93 → 0.54 → 0.19 (sev 2→3→4), mimi acoustic likewise; log-mel stays ≥0.95 because the killed band dominates the sum vector, linearizing over the interaction. Sub-additivity here is fidelity to signal physics, not representational failure — encodec's clean monotone drop is effectively an interaction-strength readout.

The argmax probe reports the dominant leg, never both. noise+clip → “noise” ~100% in every candidate (nobody says clip); hum+mp3 splits between its legs (either-leg 0.89–1.00). And on hiss+bandlimit, the masking is visible:

hiss ≈ 0 everywhere except log-mel (0.37) — after severe bandlimiting the hiss evidence is mostly gone, so “bandlimit” is arguably the perceptually correct single answer. The leak goes to “mp3”, itself a band-limiting artifact. mimi:semantic scores either-leg 0.45/0.43 on the spectral pairs — the negative control failing on its third independent instrument.
Implication for the wheel: single-label argmax collapses to the dominant artifact by construction — simultaneous reporting needs multi-label reads; and interacting pairs need either an explicit interaction term or the sub-additivity signal itself as a feature. Descriptive analysis, no pre-registered gate; A-then-B order does not commute.
