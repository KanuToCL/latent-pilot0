**The idea.** Edge speech **denoising / speaker-separation** is a large, real
need for hearables and AR glasses (on-device, low-latency, low-power). After
years of refinement, the monetisable asset would be a small, causal,
array-aware model plus the **data + evaluation harness** built here — packaged
as a patent and/or a working on-SoC demo of interest to a large hardware
multinational. What pilot0 legitimately contributes as groundwork: the
degradation library and labelled corpus (training/eval data), the probe +
quality head (a cheap perceptual evaluator and a possible distillation signal),
and representation knowledge (which latents encode which defects). The product
itself is a **separate track** with its own tiny, streaming, mic-array +
own-voice-prior architecture — the frozen-codec latent-subtraction method is a
measurement tool and an interpretability demo, **not** the edge product, and a
bare algorithm/paper is not what gets acquired (a team + data + a running demo
on the buyer's silicon is). **IP caveat to resolve first:** employment
invention-assignment obligations determine whether such a spinoff is even yours
to sell — settle publication/IP terms up front (the proposal's
industrial-doctorate route already contemplates this).
