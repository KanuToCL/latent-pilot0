Emerging Picture

pre-results — patterns and trends across three instruments, before the confirmatory arms. Companion to FIRST_LIGHT.html (the full story). Sources: gate1_real.json · geometry_real.json · combos_real.json.

Erratum, 2026-09-09. A science spot check retracted or downgraded several readings behind these trends — the severity scorecard, the “x null” concentration multiplier, the additivity cosine read alone, and DAC's “pre-quant z”. Read this page against docs/DECISIONS.md § “Science spot check — corrections” (2026-09-09), which states per claim what stands.

What we are looking for: one frozen latent whose geometry supports a differentiable “metric wheel” — a readout that reports WHAT is wrong (damage type), HOW MUCH (severity), and decomposes when two damages stack. Nothing here is confirmatory — single split, content-controlled corpus — but three independent instruments now point the same way, and that agreement is the pre-result.

1 · The map: the wanted quadrant is still empty

x = type-reading margin over the log-mel floor (Gate 1); y = severity families where the latent beats the plain energy floor; bubble size = shift-vector concentration vs the isotropic null (RQ2, median family). wavlm:l1 is the only candidate right of the floor (+0.056) but reads severity in just 2/7 families; encodec z leads severity (3/7, tied family-wide) but sits -0.101 on type. mimi:semantic (-0.549, 0/7, concentration 2.2×) is literally off this chart. No single latent does both jobs — that is the finding, not a failure of the method.

2 · Trend one: the damage signal lives shallow

All three measurements decay together through WavLM's stack: type margin +0.056 → -0.028 (l24 lands exactly on the log-mel floor), severity families 2 → 0, and the interaction read 0.64 →  0.11. The trend is consistent with SSL training doing its job — deep layers discard channel/damage information to keep content. If it holds: the wheel taps early layers or shallow codec latents; anything “semantic” is the wrong shelf. (Only encodec24k:z is pre-quantization among the swept codec variants — dac44k:z is the quantizer output; DAC's pre-quant point is the new dac44k:enc, not yet swept.)

3 · Trend two: codec acoustic latents read the physics

Phase 7's interacting pair (bandlimit erases the hiss band) sorts the field: mimi:acoustic (0.83) and encodec z (0.81) track the physical interaction hardest, while log-mel (0.05) and deep WavLM (0.11) linearize over it. Same split as the severity axis — reconstruction-trained acoustic latents preserve signal physics that classification-friendly features discard. Sub-additivity is information: a candidate interaction-strength feature for the wheel, free of charge.

5 · What the trends predict (falsifiable, before the runs)

Written down now so the queued arms can confirm or kill them — not run yet, awaiting go.

P1 — content-general corpus (VCTK restage): the log-mel floor drops once the content shortcut is removed, and wavlm:l1's type margin widens. If instead the margin collapses, the type pass was content leakage and Gate 1's one pass dies. watch: floor macro-F1 (now 0.906) and margin_over_floor for wavlm:l1 (now +0.056).
P2 — confirmation split: wavlm:l1 type F1 and encodec z's 3/7 severity hold within their bootstrap CIs on held-out groups. watch: CI overlap; analysis-only, no new encodes.
P3 — Gate 2 perceptual anchors (ViSQOL/PESQ/MOS): encodec-family severity tracks perceptual scores where plain energy fails (clip, mp3, dropout); log-mel severity mostly rides energy. watch: per-family correlation delta vs the energy baseline.
P4 — wheel architecture: the single-latent single-head wheel is trending dead. The trending design is a fusion read — wavlm:l1 for type ⊕ encodec z for severity — with multi-label heads (Phase 7: argmax collapses to the dominant leg by construction) and the sub-additivity signal as an interaction feature. watch: whether a 2-latent linear fusion clears BOTH Gate 1 bars.

6 · Read this page with these caveats

Single train/test split, no confirmation set yet; corpus is content-controlled (VCTK utterances <025), so absolute F1s are optimistic; combo analysis is descriptive with no pre-registered gate; combo order is A-then-B and does not commute; severity is largely energy-explained in 4/7 families for everyone. These are trends — the whole point of this page is to be checkable against what the confirmatory arms return.
